## Input, geometry and traversal contract

Accept exactly one operator-confirmed seed (coordinate or resolved location), a nonempty keyword, country context when required by the verified provider, and exactly one explicit finite positive radius or valid bounding box, plus finite positive grid spacing and independent work caps. Reject malformed/non-finite coordinates, invalid ranges, inverted/degenerate bounds, and antimeridian-crossing or pole-containing regions in this initial slice. Text resolution must be displayed and confirmed upstream; this document selects no geocoder. Country is not silently inferred/defaulted.

Coordinates are WGS84 latitude/longitude in decimal degrees; spacing and radius are finite positive metres. For deterministic grid placement, use a seed-anchored local equirectangular plane with fixed spherical Earth radius R=6,371,008.8 m: for integer column c and row r, x=c·spacing, y=r·spacing, latitude=lat₀+(y/R)·180/π, longitude=lon₀+(x/(R·cos(lat₀)))·180/π. Row +1 is north, column +1 east; negative indices are south/west. This defines reproducible cardinal offsets and integer identity independent of traversal order. Reject seeds at either pole (where mapping is singular), any computed center outside latitude [-90°,90°], and any grid/boundary requiring longitude wrap across ±180°; do not wrap or infer seam behavior. For bbox, reject antimeridian crossing and pole inclusion/contact before grid construction. For a radius cap, preflight independently of grid spacing: let δ=radius/R and φ=latitude in radians. Reject if δ ≥ π/2−|φ| (cap reaches or contains a pole). Otherwise its maximum longitude excursion is Δλ=asin(sin δ/cos φ); reject if Δλ ≥ π−|λ| (cap reaches or crosses the ±180° seam). This conservative initial contract rejects contact as well as crossing; it never clips or widens the requested area. These spherical-cap bounds apply for 0<δ<π/2−|φ|, guaranteed by the pole check. For radius membership and reliable-place classification use great-circle haversine distance on the same sphere (inclusive distance ≤ radius); for bbox use inclusive coordinate edges. Thus local projected spacing is exactly the configured metres, while radial eligibility is spherical rather than planar. Validate seed lies inside the requested bbox (inclusive edges) before creating grid; a radius is centered on the seed by definition. Seed is (0,0) and eligible after validation. If spacing exceeds region dimensions, other eligible centers may not exist: report queue exhaustion rather than inventing cells. Center eligibility is scheduling only, not containment of every part of a cell or returned place.

Numerical policy: Inputs are interpreted as their supplied finite decimal values; do not round geometry outward or add an epsilon to radius/bbox. Use the stated spherical formulas with stable haversine evaluation. A comparison is inclusive only when computed bounds establish ≤ (or exact bbox coordinate comparison establishes edge equality). If floating-point error bounds straddle a boundary, fail conservatively: do not enqueue a center whose eligibility cannot be established; classify a place as unverified rather than inside/outside; and reject geometry whose pole/seam preflight cannot be established as clear. This avoids silently widening the operator's area.

Persist cell identity before submission and enforce uniqueness on that identity. Duplicate neighbor discoveries do not enqueue or submit another cell. Begin with one eligible seed cell. After successful discovery with at least one raw candidate, enqueue eligible cardinal N/S/E/W neighbors only. Expansion uses raw discovery count before candidate filtering/deduplication; a duplicate-only nonempty response still permits expansion. Empty success does not expand; failure, unrecognized or uncertain response does not expand. No diagonal expansion.

For candidates with reliable coordinates, classify as inside or outside the requested area using the same inclusive radius/bounds edge rule; retain both with classification/provenance rather than silently discard outside results. Missing/unreliable coordinates are unverified, never treated as inside or outside; retain separately. Cell-center eligibility, place classification, and output retention are distinct. Coverage remains heuristic: provider omissions/caps, disconnected areas, grid spacing and stopping limits can leave gaps; connected traversal does not establish exhaustive coverage or strict place containment.

## Independent limits and durable accounting

| Limit | Unit and enforcement | Cumulative rule |
|---|---|---|
| Submission cap | Count each potentially billable provider submission at durable intent/dispatch boundary: discovery, detail, retry/resubmission, failed, empty, and uncertain sends all count. Never submit if next attempt exceeds cap. | Durable monotonically increasing session total; do not decrement on failure or restart. An uncertain send is charged conservatively. |
| Cell cap | Unique eligible cell identities admitted for processing; check before adding a new cell to queue. | Cumulative unique cells for the crawl, including seed; duplicates do not count twice. Reaching the cap blocks further admissions, not dispatch of already-admitted cells. |
| Active-time cap | Monotonic elapsed active duration; check before starting a new submission/retry and before admitting more work. | Persist accumulated active duration across sessions; never reset on cap increase. |

Active duration includes provider waits, polling, and retry backoff while the crawl is active; excludes deliberate operator pause and process downtime. Within a process use a monotonic clock, not wall-clock timestamps. Checkpoint elapsed deltas and pause/resume transitions durably. On restart, if elapsed active time since the last durable checkpoint cannot be bounded and debited conservatively, fail closed: do not admit or dispatch work until an operator supplies a defensible recovery accounting resolution. Merely disclosing unknown time is insufficient to enforce the cap. A cap increase changes only the configured ceiling; all prior totals remain. Decreases also do not erase totals.

Cap checks apply independently. Reaching the cell cap blocks further cell admissions, but already-admitted queued cells may dispatch while submission and active-time capacity remain; cell-cap exhaustion alone must not strand admitted work. A submission attempt first durably reserves one submission slot; if the resulting cumulative total is at or below the cap, that reserved attempt may dispatch. Thus the attempt that makes the total equal the cap is allowed, while the next attempt is denied. Reaching the submission cap prevents further attempts/retries. Active-time exhaustion prevents new dispatch/retry. Neither submission nor active-time caps cancel, interrupt polling, or abandon waits/backoff for already-submitted work: continue observing it, accounting active time even if this takes the total beyond the cap. Cancellation is separate; no claim is made that it cancels provider work or avoids billing. Attempt limits bound submissions, not returned/billed record count or monetary spend. Available pricing evidence is insufficient to calculate a monetary bound; leave spend unresolved pending applicable pricing evidence.

## Synthetic traces and outcome vocabulary

All trace values are synthetic, not provider evidence. State is reported distinctly as: `complete` (queue exhausted, no unresolved gaps), `partial_empty_success` (successful empty response; no expansion), `partial_failed` (explicit failure; not empty), `partial_unrecognized` (cannot classify response; not empty), `partial_limit` (a cap prevented work), `partial_cancelled`, or `partial_gaps` (queue exhausted while unresolved gaps remain). Report cell outcomes, raw candidate count, retained inside/outside/unverified candidates, remaining queue, exhausted gaps, counters and stop reason. Queue exhaustion with unresolved gaps is never `complete`.

1. **Inclusive edges and high latitude:** bbox edges are inclusive; a center exactly on an edge is eligible and a reliable place exactly on it is inside, while a place just beyond remains retained as outside. For seed (80°,0°), R=6,371,008.8 m and radius 1,000 m, the northward point at φ=80°+(1000/R)·180/π, λ=0 has haversine distance 1,000 m and is included (assuming numerical evaluation establishes ≤); a point 1,001 m due north is outside. East/west grid centers use longitude offset spacing/(R·cos 80°), not an uncorrected longitude-degree offset. The radius cap preflight yields δ≈0.000157 rad, far below pole distance π/2−80°≈0.17453 rad and seam distance π, so it passes. This illustrates spherical rather than planar-degree radius classification. Seam-crossing/contact geometry is rejected, never wrapped.
2. **Empty vs nonempty:** seed returns successful raw `[]` → `partial_empty_success`, no neighbors. A successful raw `[A]` → admit eligible cardinal neighbors, even if later filtering retains no candidate.
3. **Duplicate:** seed raw `[A]`, four eligible neighbors; same cell discovered twice → one queued/submitted cell by row/column identity. If neighbor later returns duplicate A, retain one canonical place record while preserving source-cell provenance; raw-nonempty still allows expansion.
4. **Unverified:** raw candidate U lacks trustworthy coordinates → retain in unverified set, not inside/outside; raw nonempty permits expansion.
5. **Rejected geometry preflight:** bbox crossing/contact with ±180° or containing/contacting a pole is rejected before queue creation. Radius cap centered at latitude 80° with angular radius δ=π/2−80° reaches a pole and is rejected, regardless of grid spacing. At latitude 0°, radius R·(π−|λ₀|) reaches the longitude seam and is rejected independent of spacing. No provider work/counters are consumed. A near-boundary numerical comparison whose error interval includes equality is also rejected, not expanded.
6. **Submission cap and last slot:** cap=2; reserve attempt 1 durably, then dispatch seed discovery. Reserve attempt 2 durably (total now equals cap), then dispatch the detail request; the last slot is usable. Reservation of attempt 3 is denied, and no dispatch occurs for it. Report `partial_limit`; already-submitted work may still be polled.
7. **Cell cap:** cap=2; admit seed, dispatch its discovery, then admit one unique neighbor. Further unique admissions denied; duplicates remain no-ops. The admitted neighbor is still dispatchable if submission/time caps permit, despite cell-cap exhaustion. When no admitted work remains and eligible neighbors were blocked, report `partial_limit` with blocked work recorded.
8. **Active-time cap:** cap=10 units; active work plus polling/backoff reaches 10. No further submission/retry; already-submitted result may continue to be polled, so final active duration can exceed limit and this is reported. Operator pause/downtime does not add active time; resume retains total.
9. **Failure/unrecognized/cancellation:** failed or unrecognized seed response yields respective partial state and no expansion, unlike empty success. Cancellation yields `partial_cancelled`; already-submitted work uncertainty is reported. If queue empties but unresolved gaps exist, `partial_gaps` takes precedence over complete.
10. **Crash accounting:** active-time cap=10; durable total=9 when the process crashes. If downtime is known and active interval can be bounded, debit that interval conservatively before resuming. If the post-checkpoint active interval is unknown/unbounded, restart fails closed with no admission or dispatch until accounting is resolved; reporting uncertainty alone cannot permit work.
11. **Seed/grid validation:** bbox excludes the confirmed seed → reject before queue creation/submission. Otherwise seed is (0,0) and eligible. For a tiny bbox with spacing larger than its dimensions, no cardinal center may qualify; seed runs, then queue exhaustion is reported without manufacturing extra centers.
