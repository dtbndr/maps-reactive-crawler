## Input, geometry and traversal contract

Accept exactly one operator-confirmed seed (coordinate or resolved location), a nonempty keyword, country context when required by the verified provider, and exactly one explicit finite positive radius or valid bounding box, plus finite positive grid spacing and independent work caps. Reject malformed/non-finite coordinates, invalid ranges, inverted/degenerate bounds, and antimeridian-crossing or pole-containing regions in this initial slice. Text resolution must be displayed and confirmed upstream; this document selects no geocoder. Country is not silently inferred/defaulted.

Coordinates are WGS84 latitude/longitude in decimal degrees; spacing and radius are finite positive metres. Use ordinary JavaScript Number arithmetic throughout. For deterministic grid placement, use a seed-anchored local equirectangular plane with R=6,371,008.8 m: integer column c and row r have x=c·spacing, y=r·spacing, latitude=lat₀+(y/R)·180/π, longitude=lon₀+(x/(R·cos(lat₀)))·180/π. Row +1 is north, column +1 east; negative indices are south/west. This defines reproducible cardinal offsets and integer identity independent of traversal order. Reject seeds at either pole, computed centers outside latitude [-90°,90°], and geometry requiring longitude wrap across ±180°; do not wrap. Preflight invalid, pole-contact/containing, and seam-contact/crossing bbox or radius geometry using ordinary arithmetic before queue creation. For radius membership and reliable-place classification, compute spherical haversine distance on the same sphere and include when computed distance ≤ radius. For bbox use inclusive comparisons on actual submitted coordinates (center for scheduling, place for candidate classification). Do not add arbitrary epsilon or buffer and do not round coordinates after the check. Validate seed inside bbox before grid creation; radius is centered on seed. Seed is (0,0) and eligible after validation. If spacing exceeds region dimensions, other eligible centers may not exist: report queue exhaustion rather than inventing cells. Center eligibility is scheduling only, not containment of every part of a cell or returned place.

Numerical policy: ordinary finite JavaScript Number operations and their direct comparison results define practical behavior. No certified error bounds, arbitrary tolerance, epsilon/buffer, exact-decimal model, or post-check coordinate rounding. Candidates whose coordinates are unreliable remain unverified; reliable candidates are classified inside/outside by the computed inclusive comparison.

Persist cell identity before submission and enforce uniqueness on that identity. Duplicate neighbor discoveries do not enqueue or submit another cell. Begin with one eligible seed cell. After successful discovery with at least one raw candidate, enqueue eligible cardinal N/S/E/W neighbors only. Expansion uses raw discovery count before candidate filtering/deduplication; a duplicate-only nonempty response still permits expansion. Empty success does not expand; failure, unrecognized or uncertain response does not expand. No diagonal expansion.

For candidates with reliable coordinates, classify as inside or outside the requested area using the same inclusive radius/bounds edge rule; retain both with classification/provenance rather than silently discard outside results. Missing/unreliable coordinates are unverified, never treated as inside or outside; retain separately. Cell-center eligibility, place classification, and output retention are distinct. Coverage remains heuristic: provider omissions/caps, disconnected areas, grid spacing and stopping limits can leave gaps; connected traversal does not establish exhaustive coverage or strict place containment.

## Independent limits and durable accounting

| Limit | Unit and enforcement | Cumulative rule |
|---|---|---|
| Submission cap | Count each potentially billable provider submission at durable intent/dispatch boundary: discovery, detail, retry/resubmission, failed, empty, and uncertain sends all count. Never submit if next attempt exceeds cap. | Durable monotonically increasing session total; do not decrement on failure or restart. An uncertain send is charged conservatively. |
| Cell cap | Unique eligible cell identities admitted for processing; check before adding a new cell to queue. | Cumulative unique cells for the crawl, including seed; duplicates do not count twice. Reaching the cap blocks further admissions, not dispatch of already-admitted cells. |
| Active-time cap | Monotonic elapsed active duration; check before starting a new submission/retry and before admitting more work. | Persist accumulated active duration across sessions; never reset on cap increase. |

Active duration includes provider waits, polling, and retry backoff while the crawl is active; excludes deliberate operator pause and process downtime. Within a process use a monotonic clock, not wall-clock timestamps. Checkpoint elapsed deltas and pause/resume transitions durably. On restart, if elapsed active time since the last durable checkpoint cannot be bounded and debited conservatively, fail closed: do not admit or dispatch work until an operator supplies a defensible recovery accounting resolution. Merely disclosing unknown time is insufficient to enforce the cap. A cap increase changes only the configured ceiling; all prior totals remain. Decreases also do not erase totals.

Cap checks apply independently. Reaching the cell cap blocks further cell admissions, but already-admitted queued cells may dispatch while submission and active-time capacity remain; cell-cap exhaustion alone must not strand admitted work. A submission attempt first durably reserves one submission slot; if the resulting cumulative total is at or below the cap, that reserved attempt may dispatch. Thus the attempt that makes the total equal the cap is allowed, while the next attempt is denied. Reaching the submission cap prevents further attempts/retries. Active-time exhaustion prevents new dispatch/retry. Neither submission nor active-time caps cancel, interrupt polling, or abandon waits/backoff for already-submitted work: continue observing it, accounting active time even if this takes the total beyond the cap. Cancellation is separate; no claim is made that it cancels provider work or avoids billing. Attempt limits bound submissions, not returned/billed record count or monetary spend. Available pricing evidence is insufficient to calculate a monetary bound; leave spend unresolved pending applicable pricing evidence.

## Synthetic traces and outcome vocabulary

All trace values are synthetic, not provider evidence. State is reported distinctly as: `complete` (queue exhausted, no unresolved gaps), `partial_empty_success` (successful empty response; no expansion), `partial_failed` (explicit failure; not empty), `partial_unrecognized` (cannot classify response; not empty), `partial_limit` (a cap prevented work), `partial_cancelled`, or `partial_gaps` (queue exhausted while unresolved gaps remain). Report cell outcomes, raw candidate count, retained inside/outside/unverified candidates, remaining queue, exhausted gaps, counters and stop reason. Queue exhaustion with unresolved gaps is never `complete`.

1. **Inclusive edges and high latitude:** bbox edges are inclusive; a center exactly on an edge is eligible and a reliable place exactly on it is inside, while a place just beyond remains retained as outside. For seed (80°,0°), R=6,371,008.8 m and radius 1,000 m, the northward point at φ=80°+(1000/R)·180/π, λ=0 has haversine distance 1,000 m and is included (assuming numerical evaluation establishes ≤); a point 1,001 m due north is outside. East/west grid centers use longitude offset spacing/(R·cos 80°), not an uncorrected longitude-degree offset. The radius cap preflight yields δ≈0.000157 rad, far below pole distance π/2−80°≈0.17453 rad and seam distance π, so it passes. This illustrates spherical rather than planar-degree radius classification. Seam-crossing/contact geometry is rejected, never wrapped.
2. **Empty vs nonempty:** seed returns successful raw `[]` → `partial_empty_success`, no neighbors. A successful raw `[A]` → admit eligible cardinal neighbors, even if later filtering retains no candidate.
3. **Duplicate:** seed raw `[A]`, four eligible neighbors; same cell discovered twice → one queued/submitted cell by row/column identity. If neighbor later returns duplicate A, retain one canonical place record while preserving source-cell provenance; raw-nonempty still allows expansion.
4. **Unverified:** raw candidate U lacks trustworthy coordinates → retain in unverified set, not inside/outside; raw nonempty permits expansion.
5. **Rejected geometry preflight:** bbox crossing/contact with ±180° or containing/contacting a pole is rejected before queue creation. Radius cap centered at latitude 80° with angular radius δ=π/2−80° reaches a pole and is rejected, regardless of grid spacing. At latitude 0°, radius R·(π−|λ₀|) reaches the longitude seam and is rejected independent of spacing. No provider work/counters are consumed. A borderline preflight follows the direct ordinary-arithmetic comparison; no separate uncertainty interval or tolerance is applied.
6. **Submission cap and last slot:** cap=2; reserve attempt 1 durably, then dispatch seed discovery. Reserve attempt 2 durably (total now equals cap), then dispatch the detail request; the last slot is usable. Reservation of attempt 3 is denied, and no dispatch occurs for it. Report `partial_limit`; already-submitted work may still be polled.
7. **Cell cap:** cap=2; admit seed, dispatch its discovery, then admit one unique neighbor. Further unique admissions denied; duplicates remain no-ops. The admitted neighbor is still dispatchable if submission/time caps permit, despite cell-cap exhaustion. When no admitted work remains and eligible neighbors were blocked, report `partial_limit` with blocked work recorded.
8. **Active-time cap:** cap=10 units; active work plus polling/backoff reaches 10. No further submission/retry; already-submitted result may continue to be polled, so final active duration can exceed limit and this is reported. Operator pause/downtime does not add active time; resume retains total.
9. **Failure/unrecognized/cancellation:** failed or unrecognized seed response yields respective partial state and no expansion, unlike empty success. Cancellation yields `partial_cancelled`; already-submitted work uncertainty is reported. If queue empties but unresolved gaps exist, `partial_gaps` takes precedence over complete.
10. **Crash accounting:** active-time cap=10; durable total=9 when the process crashes. If downtime is known and active interval can be bounded, debit that interval conservatively before resuming. If the post-checkpoint active interval is unknown/unbounded, restart fails closed with no admission or dispatch until accounting is resolved; reporting uncertainty alone cannot permit work.
11. **Seed/grid validation:** bbox excludes the confirmed seed → reject before queue creation/submission. Otherwise seed is (0,0) and eligible. For a tiny bbox with spacing larger than its dimensions, no cardinal center may qualify; seed runs, then queue exhaustion is reported without manufacturing extra centers.

## Slice B: Recovery, failure handling and provider unknowns

**Offline acceptance contract; synthetic traces only.** This section extends Slice A's durable counters and outcome vocabulary. It is not runtime behavior, a provider guarantee, or authorization for paid/live calls. Failure dispositions below are conservative local accounting rules, not a selected provider retry policy.

### Failure classes, gaps and operator decisions

Treat a cell-discovery failure as isolated only when evidence establishes that the failure belongs to that cell and does not invalidate session state, credentials, or accounting. An isolated failure is recorded against that cell; do not expand from it. Other already-admitted independent cells may continue while limits permit. A global failure, corrupted/untrustworthy checkpoint, ambiguous session-wide authorization/configuration, or inability to account for elapsed time pauses all new work. Preserve outstanding IDs and uncertainty; do not relabel failure as empty success.

Retry only a known-safe transient condition, with a finite configured retry ceiling and bounded backoff, and only when the provider contract establishes that retrying cannot create duplicate paid work. Every potentially billable submission—including each retry—first consumes a durable submission reservation. Unknown acceptance/billing is not known-safe transient: pause the affected work for operator resolution. Each unresolved failure creates a durable gap with scope (cell discovery or candidate detail), cause/class, associated candidate/place identity if known, attempt history, and status. A gap is resolved only by verified completion or valid same-identity reconciliation. An explicitly approved resend authorizes an additional attempt, not gap resolution; keep the gap open until its result is verified. An operator-selected skip closes workflow handling but preserves a visible gap in the final report. Resume never clears counters or gaps. A cap increase changes the ceiling only; it cannot reset attempts, cells, active time, or retry count. Require a detail request to be separately budgeted and checkpointed.

Maintain durable retry accounting separately from submission reservations. For each work identity and operation (cell discovery, candidate detail, or observation of a known ID), checkpoint retry ordinal and configured finite ceiling before scheduling each retry; persist the increment before dispatch/wait so a crash cannot replenish allowance. Restore the maximum durable ordinal after restart; do not decrement on failure, pause, cap change, or uncertain result. Observation retries (poll/fetch for an already-known ID) consume retry allowance but do not increment S; a new potentially billable submission/resubmission consumes both its applicable retry ordinal and a fresh S reservation before send. Initial attempts are ordinal zero. Automatic retries are permitted only for known-safe transients within their durable ceiling; when exhausted, preserve a gap and pause that item. Operator-authorized resends are an exception to automatic-retry safety: explicit approval authorizes the duplicate-risk action but does not establish provider safety, remove billing uncertainty, or resolve the gap. Record approval, rationale, and a durable resend ordinal before action; apply the configured finite resend ceiling as well as the submission cap, and reserve a fresh S slot. Unknown acceptance is never made safe by remaining automatic retry allowance.

Deduplicate cells by stable cell identity and candidates/places by a verified canonical identity when available; retain source-cell and candidate provenance. A discovery gap is cell-scoped and must not be erased merely because another cell discovers the same place. Merge duplicate candidate observations to one canonical place, preserve provenance, and reconcile candidate-level detail gaps to that canonical identity: a verified successful detail result for that same identity can close a duplicate detail gap; a different or uncertain identity cannot. Report unreconciled cell gaps and detail gaps separately. A cell may have successful discovery and still yield unresolved candidate detail gaps.

All IDs, counters and outcomes here are synthetic. `S` is the durable cumulative potentially-billable submission count; it never decreases. Before each new potentially billable send, checkpoint intent and reserve its slot, incrementing S. A durable intent may over-count an unsent attempt after crash; this is intentional conservative accounting. No trace asserts provider idempotency, lookup, billing, or cancellation support.

If an uncertain send used the third slot, no automatic resend is allowed under the original cap. A newly approved resend consumes a new slot and still cannot exceed the current cap. Operator approval resolves authorization to act, not provider billing uncertainty.

### Cancellation, time stop, and terminal reporting

If cancellation occurs after a send may have been accepted, stop admitting new cells/submissions, checkpoint local cancellation, S and intent/ID state, and retain outstanding uncertainty. Do not claim provider cancellation, non-billing, or successful completion. If the active-time cap is reached, stop new admissions, submissions, and submission-retries but continue observing already-submitted work (including contract-safe observation retries within their durable ceiling) and account for additional active time, as Slice A explicitly requires; the time cap alone does not abandon observation. Cancellation is distinct and may stop local observation. On restart restore uncertainty and cumulative counters. If active elapsed time since durable checkpoint is unbounded, fail closed as Slice A specifies until defensible accounting resolution; do not erase the uncertain send.

Report partial outcome with per-cell/per-candidate statuses, queue remaining or exhausted, unresolved gap inventory, S and cap, unique cell count and cap, active time and cap, and stop reason. If queue exhaustion leaves unresolved gaps, outcome is `partial_gaps`, never `complete`. If work stops at a limit with gaps, report the limiting stop and unresolved gaps rather than hiding one status; `partial_limit` plus gap details is acceptable. Cancellation is `partial_cancelled` with any outstanding uncertainty explicitly itemized. An operator skip may allow queue processing to continue but final status remains partial with the skipped gap.

### Provider evidence and integration blockers

The [Bright Data research](bright-data-mcp.md), with public sources checked 2026-11-12, documents MCP's `run_scraper` up-to-45-second wait and possible snapshot ID, progress labels (`starting`, `running`, `ready`, `failed`, `canceled`), and result retrieval for finished runs. General direct API documentation describes snapshot IDs for long-running requests and polling hints; this is not proof of MCP parity. MCP tool descriptions say runs are billed per record. The same research explicitly finds no documented submission idempotency key, exactly-once guarantee, lookup by caller-supplied key/input, lost-ack recovery, duplicate suppression, or duplicate billing adjustment. No source establishes whether cancellation is available for this workflow, its race/billing semantics, whether failed polling/fetch implies anything about run state, or whether failed/partial/canceled work is billed. These are documentation observations, not claims that the provider lacks such features.

**Integration blockers requiring current authoritative documentation or provider confirmation before a paid integration:** (1) submission acknowledgment, durable ID, client correlation and lookup/recovery after lost acknowledgment; (2) idempotency and duplicate suppression across retries; (3) retryable errors, partial/empty distinctions, poll/fetch failure semantics and finite retry safety; (4) cancellation operation, race behavior, terminal states and billing; (5) exact billing units/charges for discovery, detail, failed, empty, retry, duplicate, partial and canceled runs; (6) retention/availability and lookup of known IDs. Existing evidence does not resolve these. Resolve documentary evidence first; any live probe requires separate operator approval and cannot prove account billing terms or legal rights. No provider call, payment, publication or account change is authorized here.
